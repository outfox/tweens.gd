## Units and constraints

- Rotation uses radians; camera `Fov` and light angles use degrees.
- `Tweens.GlobalQuaternion3D` interpolates along the shortest quaternion path,
  then writes Godot's YXZ global rotation. It preserves the global position and
  basis scale. Like Godot's `GlobalRotation`, it replaces shear, and it can change
  local scale under a nonuniformly scaled parent.
- `GeometryInstance3D.Transparency` is an amount of transparency (0 opaque,
  1 transparent), not opacity. Renderer support and sorting are Godot's.
- `Camera3D` `Size` and `FrustumOffset` depend on the projection mode, and
  `LightTemperature` on physical light settings.
- Particle `SpeedScale` and `Lifetime` are `double`. Emission parameters depend on
  the configured emitter modes and materials.
- Path definitions require a `PathFollow3D` under a `Path3D` with a nonempty
  curve. The node still controls looping and wrapping.
